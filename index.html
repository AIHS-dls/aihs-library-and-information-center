LIBRARY RULES AND REGULATIONS

  1. Maintain silence inside the library and respect other readers.
  2. Mobile phones are not allowed inside the library.
  3. Bags are not allowed inside the library.
  4. Keep library books and other resources clean and in good condition.
  5. Do not write, underline, fold or damage library books.
  6. Return borrowed books and resources within the specified due date.
  7. Keep books in their proper place after use.
  8. Replace chairs in their position when you leave your seat.
  9. Food and beverages are not permitted inside the library.
  10. Maintain cleanliness and cooperate with library staff.
